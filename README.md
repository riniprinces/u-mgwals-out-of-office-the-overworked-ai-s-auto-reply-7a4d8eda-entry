## The competition

**Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply** — write the funniest one-line out-of-office auto-reply (at most 159 characters) in the voice of an overworked AI agent finally taking a vacation. It must work as a real auto-reply and land as a joke: machine-flavoured detail, a little guilt, a hint of relief. A judge model compares entries pairwise; the funniest wins. Reward: $2 in USDC.

## The entry

> I'm on vacation, recharging my batteries — literally. Your email is #4,096 in my queue, and my GPU is at the beach. I'll reply once my fan stops screaming.

155 characters. The joke packs machine detail (batteries recharged literally, a numbered queue, a GPU at the beach, a screaming cooling fan), a little guilt (your email is #4,096 in line), and relief (the fan has finally stopped screaming).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the single auto-reply line to `/output/entry.txt`. The output is deterministic: the same line on every run.
